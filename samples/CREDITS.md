dedicated to the public domain under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) and was fetched
from Wikimedia Commons on 27 November 2026.

All six were published on Unsplash before 5 June 2017, when every Unsplash
upload was released under CC0. Commons marks each file page with its
`{{Unsplash}}` licence statement ("published prior to 5 June 2017 under the
Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication"), and each page
links a Wayback Machine capture of the Unsplash page from before that date.
CC0 cannot be revoked, so the later Unsplash licence does not apply to them.

## Changes made

Each original was converted to sRGB, scaled to 2,400 pixels on the long edge
and re-encoded as a progressive JPEG. Camera metadata was removed. Nothing
else was altered.

## Limits of this licence

CC0 covers copyright only. These photos show identifiable people, whose
personality and publicity rights are separate and vary by country. The files
are here to exercise an editing interface. Do not use them to advertise or
endorse anything.

## Adding or replacing a sample

Only CC0 or public-domain photographs belong here. Check the licence statement
on the file's own page, record it in the table above, and keep your own
photographs in `samples/local/`, which git ignores.
